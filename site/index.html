All product names, brands and logos belong to their respective owners.

There is no official SDK behind this. Everything the relay does was worked out from the device's own public API documentation, from prior community research, and from watching a real clock respond. It targets what was verified on an LM 37X8 running firmware 2.3.9; a firmware update may change behaviour at any time.

This page is a simulation, not a photograph. The geometry and every frame are generated from the code, but LaMetric publishes no font file, so the glyphs are a reproduction drawn to match the proportions of a real device. Frame duration and scroll speed are undocumented and tuned by eye. Icons are the work of their authors and are fetched from the LaMetric community library at build time. They are reproduced here to demonstrate the display, they carry no licence from this project, and any author who would rather not appear can have theirs removed by opening an issue.
